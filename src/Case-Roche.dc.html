Roche personalized healthcare campaign

A campaign site that explains personalized health to the public through an interactive network of health elements. It ran for five years and was extended several times.

Client
Roche
Country
Switzerland
Industry
Healthcare, pharma
Year
2018–2023
My role
Project lead and UX designer
Team
A UI designer on the visuals

Problem

Personalized health was largely unknown to the general public.

  • A complex medical idea for a general audience
  • It had to show what personalized care offers compared with generalized care

Approach

Every decision traces back to evidence. Each step has an ID, and every insight and decision names the evidence it came from.

  1. S001

    Source

    Campaign brief

    Make personalized health understandable to the public.

  2. E001

    Evidence

    An unknown idea

    Most people had never heard of personalized health.

    From S001

  3. I001

    Insight

    Show the connections

    Personalized health links many health factors together.

    From E001

  4. D001

    Decision

    A neural network

    Health elements as connected, animated nodes.

    From I001

  5. D002

    Decision

    Click to compare

    Each node shows the benefits of personalized versus generalized care.

    From I001

Solution

I led the project and the UX. A UI designer did the visuals.

A network of health elements

Health elements appear as connected, animated nodes, like a neural network. Click a node to see the benefits of personalized versus generalized healthcare. The campaign also introduced artificial intelligence as a core part of personalized healthcare.

Outcome

The campaign ran for five years and was extended several times because of its success. Reach numbers were not shared, but each extension showed the concept kept working for the client.
