Human Rights Guide

A text-only, color-coded guide that explains human rights to the public. Built to expand, it grew from 1 to 12 countries and won the Erasmus+ Quality Award 2024.

Client
Baltic Human Rights Society
Country
EU
Industry
NGO, education
Year
2022–2025
My role
UX and UI designer
Team
An external strategist, a UI designer and a PM

Problem

Human rights had to be understandable to people without legal knowledge, in a text-only format that is easy to roll out to new countries.

  • Citizens and inhabitants: rights holders in everyday situations
  • Professionals
  • Educators and learners

Solution

I designed the UX and the UI, working with an external strategist, a UI designer and a project manager.

Eight main topics

People learn about human rights through themes and daily-life situations. Topics include:

  • What human rights are
  • Freedom of expression and media
  • Discrimination
  • Domestic violence
  • Hate crimes and hate speech
  • Human-rights institutions and organizations that can help

A color system

  • Light blue for themes, also the identity color
  • Green for rights
  • Pink for case law
  • Orange for knowledge tests

Outcome

The guide grew from 1 country to 12. Co-funded by the European Union, it won the Erasmus+ Quality Award 2024.
