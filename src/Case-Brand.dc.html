Work · Aviation

Loft Dynamics website and brand

A focused brand refresh, from navy to sky blue and light to dark, and a leaner website built around four customer profiles.

Client
Loft Dynamics
Country
Switzerland
Industry
Aviation, VR flight simulation
Year
2024–2026
My role
Project lead, UX and UI designer
Team
A front-end and a back-end developer; a PM on the client side

Problem

Loft Dynamics sells to very different people, from helicopter operators and pilots to aircraft manufacturers and airlines. Its brand and website spoke to none of them in particular.

  • A product-centric look, in the navy blue the whole industry uses
  • A website too heavy for audiences with very different needs

Solution

I led the brand refresh and the website redesign and did the work myself, from the stakeholder interviews to the trade-show look, with a front-end and a back-end developer on the build and a project manager at the client.

Brand: a light facelift

The owl logo stayed. Everything around it moved from the sea to the sky, and from light to dark.

  • Navy blue became sky blue
  • Light mode became dark mode, so the white simulator stands out
  • Trade-show boxing, packaging and backdrops in dark mode, using dark VR scenes of the flight environment
  • A redesigned trade-show outfit for the team
  • A trade-show booking platform set up on Microsoft Bookings

Website: built around the audiences

Fewer pages and a single-level menu. Products show the three simulators; Solutions show what each audience gets for its own needs.

Loft Fest: the main stage

The brand also carried Loft Dynamics’ annual Loft Fest. I helped with the visuals, including animations and videos for the main stage screen.

Outcome

The brand was well received: more serious, yet more dynamic. At trade shows, sky blue now stands apart from the navy used across the industry.
