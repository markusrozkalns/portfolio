Theapolis platform and brand

Redesigned and rebranded the professional network for theater people in Germany, Austria and Switzerland, with research the client said shaped their business.

Client
Theapolis
Country
Germany
Industry
Culture, professional network
Year
2016–2017
My role
Project lead, UX and UI designer
Team
A front-end developer and a PM on the client side

Problem

Theapolis connects the theater industry: actors, directors, technicians and more. The platform needed a redesign and a clearer identity.

  • Many different professions on one network
  • The client’s in-house developers would maintain it for the long term
  • An outdated visual identity

Solution

I led the project and did the UX, the UI and the rebrand, with a front-end developer on the build and a project manager at the client.

Platform

  • Job offers
  • Personal CVs linked to specific productions
  • Click a play to see its cast and staff
  • A design system for the client’s in-house developers

Rebrand

Logo, colors, visual language and tone of voice.

Outcome

The platform has served theater people in Germany, Austria and Switzerland on this design for several years. The client said the interviews alone, even without the redesign, helped shape their business for future growth.
